# DSO Tariff Map – Danish DSO tariff map

Interactive map of Danish distribution system operator (DSO) network tariffs, 2014–2026, for households and business customer classes, plus a separate **"Filed for 2027 – may be revised"** layer showing only prices DSOs have already filed in Datahub for 2027 (grey = not yet filed; nothing estimated).

A third, separate view, **"Investment outlook – DSO network development plan 2027–2036"** (toggle at the top of the sidebar), shows each DSO's *planned* grid investment per meter relative to the national median. Source: the DSOs' network development plans (netudviklingsplaner), as published by Forsyningstilsynet. These are **investment plans, not tariffs**: consultation drafts (May–Aug 2026), not converted to øre/kWh, and not a price forecast. Final plans are due in December 2026.

**Live map:** https://notjakobkristensen.github.io/DSO-Tariff-Map/

Data: Energinet Datahub price lists (Energi Data Service), refreshed weekly. Research by Jakob Kristensen and Georgios Fotis (Aarhus University).
